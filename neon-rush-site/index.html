Game rules

How to play

Every race is 38 km of chaos. Here's what you need to know.

  1. Race to the finish. 20 cars start together. Cross the finish line in the best position you can.
  2. Every car has 100 HP. Bullets, missiles, mines, crashes and enemies all take HP away.
  3. Your guns fire automatically. Steer to aim at rivals and enemies ahead of you. You can switch auto-fire off and fire manually.
  4. Grab weapon crates (?). Each gives a random weapon (plasma, missile or mine) to fire with the weapon button.
  1. Watch the road ahead. Enemy drones shoot at you and armored trucks drive the wrong way. Destroy them or dodge.
  2. Destroyed? You respawn. Your car explodes and comes back after 2 seconds with full HP, but you lose places.
  3. Use pickups. Repair restores HP, Shield blocks 50 damage, and Nitro gives you a speed burst.
  4. Play fair. One player per device. Cheating or hacked scores are removed from the leaderboard and can't receive rewards.

Arsenal & pickups

Weapons and power-ups

✛

Twin Guns

Always on. Fast twin shots for 5 damage each. Unlimited ammo.

◆

Plasma Cannon

10 rapid plasma bolts, 20 damage each. Great for finishing off rivals.

➤

Homing Missile

3 missiles that lock onto the nearest target ahead. 40 damage each.

✸

Proximity Mine

Drop 3 mines behind you. 35 damage to anyone who drives over one.

✚

Repair Kit

Instantly restores 35 HP.

◯

Energy Shield & Nitro

The Shield absorbs the next 50 damage. Nitro gives a 2.5-second speed burst.

⚠️ Enemies coming from the front

🤖

Attack Drone

Flies toward you and fires red energy bolts. 45 HP. Ramming one costs you 20 HP. Destroy it for +30 points.

🚛

Armored Wrong-Way Truck

A 160 HP armored hauler driving straight at the pack. A head-on hit costs 40 HP. Shoot it down or dodge.

FAQ

Questions

Is Neon Rush free?

Yes. It's completely free and runs in your browser on phone or PC. There's nothing to download.

Do I need a crypto wallet to play?

No. Just enter a pilot name and race. A wallet address will only be needed later if you want to receive $CANDLE rewards.

How is my rank saved?

Your best score is saved to the global leaderboard under your pilot name. Your device remembers you, so keep using the same browser to keep your rank.

Are the other racers real players?

Right now the 19 rivals are AI pilots, and you compete with real players through the global leaderboard. Live multiplayer is on the roadmap.

What is $CANDLE?

$CANDLE is our community token on the vibe/vibe testnet (Robinhood Chain). Testnet tokens have no real-money value. This is not financial advice.
